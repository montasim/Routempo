# Replacement name research for RoutineFlow

Checked: 2026-08-10

## Recommendation

**Lead with `Routempo`.** It is short, pronounceable, and joins the two ideas at
the center of the product: routines and time. It distinguishes the product from
task planners without making the brand feel clinical, and it can naturally own
the contrast between scheduled time and actual execution time.

**Keep `CadenceTrace` as the clearest descriptive alternative and
`Routinometry` as the most ownable one.** CadenceTrace expresses repeated timing
plus a historical record; Routinometry literally suggests “measurement of
routines,” at the cost of pronunciation and length.

Before shipping either name, run a formal trademark clearance and reserve the
store listing, domain, and social handles together. The checks below are a
current collision screen, not a legal opinion or a guarantee that Google Play
will accept a title.

## What the name needs to communicate

The repository defines the product as a **behavioral measurement system, not a
to-do app**. It records scheduled behaviors, captures actual execution times,
and derives analytics from immutable routine logs
([BRD](../requiremnts.md)). The design language reinforces “Discipline,
measured,” calm factual copy, time as the medium, and data as the product's
imagery ([design-system brief](../design_system/readme.md)).

That makes these naming territories appropriate:

- cadence, timing, and repeated patterns;
- traces, records, evidence, and measurement;
- neutral observation rather than hype, guilt, or gamification.

Names centered on “flow,” “planner,” “tasks,” or “coach” are weaker because
they collapse the product back into the crowded productivity category.

## Current conflict that requires the rename

The exact name is not merely similar to another listing: Google Play currently
has **RoutineFlow: Routine for ADHD** by System Two GmbH. Its official listing
shows 500K+ downloads and substantial overlap in routine tracking, reminders,
and statistics ([Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.routineflow.routineflow)).
That is a high-confusion conflict and makes a modifier such as “RoutineFlow
Analytics” a poor fix.

## Suggested store presentation

The Google Play title should keep the distinctive brand first and use the
descriptor for discovery rather than stuffing generic words into the brand:

- **Routempo: Routine Analytics**
- Short description: **Track scheduled routines, actual completion times, and behavioral drift.**
- Brand line: **Discipline, measured.**

For the more descriptive alternative:

- **CadenceTrace: Routine Analytics**
- Short description: **See consistency, delay, and drift across every scheduled routine.**

For the more ownable coined alternative:

- **Routinometry: Routine Tracker**
- Short description: **Measure consistency, delay, streaks, and behavioral drift from every routine log.**

## Due diligence still required

1. Search exact and confusingly similar marks in the launch jurisdictions and
   relevant software/service classes; use a trademark professional for final
   clearance. A general web search is not a substitute.
2. Confirm title availability from the actual Google Play Console. Public
   search cannot expose drafts, suspended apps, unpublished listings, or every
   regional result.
3. Recheck and register the preferred domain immediately. A `404` response from
   Verisign RDAP means no current `.com` registration record was returned at
   check time; it is not a reservation.
4. Check Apple App Store, package identifiers, major social handles, and local
   company-name registries before committing design or migration work.
